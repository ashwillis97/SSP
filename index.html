Common questions

How much does it cost?

Ironing is £4 per kilo. You're charged by the weight of your ironing, not per item.

Do you collect and deliver?

Yes. We offer a full pickup and drop-off service right to your door, within a 6-mile radius of Mackworth. The minimum load for pickup and drop-off is 6 kg.

For pickup and drop-off the minimum load is 6 kg.
